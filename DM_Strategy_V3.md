# Exotiq DM Strategy V3

Updated March 23, 2026. Replaces V2.

Primary change: All fabricated statistics removed. Social proof now anchored to real operators and real outcomes only.

---

## Social Proof Framework

Every claim in outreach must fall into one of three tiers. If it doesn't fit Tier 1 or Tier 2, it does not go in a message.

### Tier 1: Real Operators, Real Outcomes (Name-Droppable)

These are verified facts about operators currently on the Exotiq platform. Use names, companies, and specifics freely.

**Denver Exotic Rentals (Jay)**
- Replaced his entire ops stack with the Command Center.
- Saving 5+ hours a week from manual tasks today.
- Estimated to hit 15+ hours saved per week when peak season starts.
- Not yet using dynamic pricing, so no revenue numbers to cite.

**Saucy Rentals (Fredo D'Lima, Miami)**
- Booked a demo after one conversation. Onboarding in progress.
- Once live, becomes another name-drop. Do NOT cite results until he's been on the platform long enough to measure them.

**How to use Tier 1:**
- "Jay at Denver Exotic Rentals replaced his entire system. Months in, already seeing massive upside."
- "Operators in Scottsdale and Denver are running everything through one dashboard now."
- "Jay went from spreadsheets and a Notes app to a full command center. He's getting five hours a week back and hasn't even hit peak season yet."

### Tier 2: Product Truths (What the Platform Actually Does)

These are honest descriptions of Exotiq's capabilities. They describe what the product does, not fabricated results from using it.

- "The pricing engine catches demand spikes from events before you'd spot them manually." (True about MotorIQ's capability.)
- "Most operators we talk to are running their fleet off spreadsheets and a Notes app. The ones who switch aren't going back."
- "We built this because we saw how much money gets left on the table during event weeks. Art Basel, fight nights, boat shows."
- "One dashboard instead of five different apps."
- "Rari handles booking calls so you don't have to."
- "Founding Member pricing locked for life." (Real offer.)
- "30-day free pilot with white-glove onboarding." (Real offer.)

**Compliance / Vault (Tier 2, but NOT a lead angle):**

The $10-50K compliance failure range is defensible. It's not one stat. It's the real cost of things going wrong when operators manage compliance manually:
- Registration lapse: Car can't legally rent. A $1,000/day car sitting during Art Basel week is $7K gone.
- Insurance gap: Full liability exposure on a $300K+ asset. Even without an incident, the car is dead inventory.
- Missed maintenance: Skipped brake job turns into a $5-15K repair plus weeks out of rotation. During peak season, that's the repair bill plus $500-1,000/day in missed bookings.
- Double booking / inventory mismatch: Client gets turned away, leaves a 1-star review, disputes the deposit.

Any one of these costs $10K easily. Stack two or three in a bad month and you're at $50K.

DO NOT lead with compliance in cold outreach. It's a fear angle, not an excitement angle. Compliance belongs in:
- Second or third touch in a sequence, not the opener.
- Demo calls, where you can walk through Vault and let the operator connect it to their own experience.
- Responses to "what else does it do?" after initial interest is established.

When you do use it, frame it as a question, not a claim: "What does it cost you when a registration lapses on a $1,000/day car during peak week?" Let the operator do the math themselves. That lands harder than handing them a number.

**How to use Tier 2:**
Be clever and confident, but never attach a specific number to a capability unless that number comes from a real operator (Tier 1). "The pricing engine catches what you'd miss" is fine. "The pricing engine adds $40K a quarter" is not.

### Tier 3: NEVER SAY (Fabricated or Unverified)

These claims were in V2 and are now permanently retired:

- ~~"18-35% revenue improvement from AI-optimized pricing"~~ -- Based on an old hypothetical model run. Not measured from a real operator. REMOVED.
- ~~"$40K+ in additional quarterly revenue"~~ -- Completely fabricated. REMOVED.
- ~~"15+ hours saved weekly on operations"~~ as a general stat -- Replace with Jay's real number: "5+ hours a week today, projected 15+ at peak season."
- ~~"$47K average cost of a single compliance failure"~~ -- Replaced with defensible $10-50K range. See Tier 2 compliance section for usage rules. Never lead with this in cold outreach.
- Any dollar figure, percentage, or time savings tied to a specific operator's results that has not been measured.
- Any "operators are seeing X%" without a named source.
- Any ROI calculation or projection presented as a proven result.

**The rule is simple:** If Gregory can't defend the number on a demo call, it doesn't go in the DM.

## DM Templates (Updated)

All templates below have been cleaned of fabricated stats. They use Tier 1 and Tier 2 proof only.

### Template B: FOMO / Social Proof
Best for: High-score leads (4-5), operators with strong IG presence, competitive markets.

Structure: Specific compliment about their operation > Name-drop a real operator > Product truth > Soft CTA.

Example:
> Hey, Gregory here. I run Exotiq. Started in the exotic rental world before building the tech.
>
> Jay at Denver Exotic Rentals just replaced his entire ops stack with our Command Center. Five hours a week back already, and he hasn't even hit peak season. We built it specifically for operators running fleets at your level.
>
> Curious how you're handling [pricing / fleet logistics / booking volume]. Worth a quick look? https://calendly.com/hello-exotiq

### Template D: Peer / Curiosity
Best for: Mid-score leads (3-4), operators who are clearly hands-on, markets where you want to start conversations.

Structure: Acknowledge what they've built > Ask a real question about their ops > Offer to compare notes > No hard CTA.

Example:
> Hey, Gregory here. I run Exotiq. Started in exotics before building the tech.
>
> [Specific thing about their fleet/IG/market]. Curious how you're handling pricing, especially with [relevant seasonal event]. That's where most operators tell us they're leaving money on the table.
>
> Connecting with operators this month. Happy to share what we're learning from the ones already on the platform. No sales pitch.

### Template E: Visual / Fleet Compliment
Best for: Operators with great IG content, recent car posts, strong visual brand.

Structure: Reference a specific post or vehicle > Connect it to a product truth > Soft CTA.

Example:
> Hey, it's Gregory at Exotiq.
>
> First off, [specific car from their feed] is unreal. [Quick personal note about the car.]
>
> I'm connecting with exotic car operators and helping them run tighter operations. With you running [fleet size] vehicles in [market], I'd love your take. Could we grab 15 minutes? https://calendly.com/hello-exotiq

### Template F: Repair / Re-engagement
Best for: Leads where previous outreach was missed, auto-replied, or dropped.

Structure: Own the dropped ball > Add new value > Re-engage with respect.

Example:
> Hey, Gregory Ringler here from Exotiq. I realized I dropped the ball on my end. You responded and I missed it. That's on me.
>
> Wanted to circle back properly. We've built a command center specifically for multi-city exotic operators. Jay at Denver Exotic Rentals went from spreadsheets to a full dashboard and hasn't looked back.
>
> Would love to reconnect if the timing works better now.

## Voice Rules (Unchanged from V2)

- No em-dashes. Ever. Use periods or commas instead.
- No generic SaaS words: "streamline," "leverage," "synergy," "circle back," "touch base."
- No desperate energy. No "I'd love to pick your brain." No "just following up!"
- Short sentences. Punchy. Respect the operator's time.
- Conversational but not sloppy.
- Never say "I hope this message finds you well."
- McLaren energy. Confident, direct, benefit-first.
- Premium. Exclusive. Invitation, not pitch.

---

## What NOT to Do (Updated)

- Don't fabricate statistics, dollar amounts, or percentages. Ever. If it isn't from a real operator on the platform, it doesn't go in the message.
- Don't present projections as proven results. "The pricing engine catches demand spikes" is honest. "Operators are seeing 18-35% revenue improvement" is not (yet).
- Don't calculate ROI estimates for prospects. We don't have enough real data to back those up on a call.
- Don't write walls of text. Operators won't read them.
- Don't list features. Lead with outcomes and real stories.
- Don't mention competitors by name (except Turo).
- Don't use exclamation points more than once per message.
- Don't include pricing in cold outreach. That's for the demo call.
- Don't say "Book a demo." Say "grab 15 minutes" or "worth a quick look."
